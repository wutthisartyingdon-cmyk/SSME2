LIVVELL ("we", "us") provides personalized meal suggestions based on food preferences, information you provide, and, with your permission, wearable health data. This policy explains how we process personal information when you use the LIVVELL mobile application.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account identifiers, meal choices and photographs you submit, your self-reported energy, sleepiness and fullness, and app usage and diagnostic information. If you explicitly connect a compatible wearable or health service, we may access the specific data types you authorize, such as sleep duration and patterns, heart rate, heart-rate variability (HRV), and activity metrics, subject to the permissions granted and data actually available.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide meal recommendations, estimate daily wellness signals such as energy and recovery, display your history, maintain and secure the service, troubleshoot issues, and improve recommendation quality. Wearable data is used only for the purposes disclosed at the time of permission and as permitted by applicable platform rules.

3. Permissions and choices

Connecting a wearable is optional. You can decline or revoke health-data permissions through the relevant device, health service, or app settings. Revoking access stops future authorized collection, but does not automatically erase data previously stored; contact us to request deletion.

4. Storage and service providers

Information may be stored and processed using our application backend and cloud service providers, including Supabase where configured. If you submit meal photographs for automated analysis, the images or related data may be sent to an AI processing provider, including OpenAI where enabled. We do not sell your health information. We require appropriate safeguards from processors and restrict access according to operational need.

5. Retention and security

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above, legal obligations, security, and legitimate service operations. We use reasonable technical and organizational security measures, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Specific retention periods and deletion procedures should be confirmed before publication.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or other available privacy rights. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling requests.

7. International processing

Our service providers may process information outside your country. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

8. Children

LIVVELL is not intended for children without any consent required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect children's health information without the required authorization.

9. Wellness disclaimer

LIVVELL offers general wellness and nutrition suggestions, not medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.
